# PRODUCT.md

**Name:** andey0saikiran.github.io (personal resume site)
**Register:** brand (portfolio; design is the product, but the product is credibility)
**Audience:** technical recruiters and hiring managers skimming between ATS tabs; secondary: engineers vetting the candidate after a referral.
**One-liner:** A data scientist's resume that behaves like his work: fast, precise, and checkable, with every claim linked to a live artifact.

**Voice words:** precise, checkable, quietly confident.
**Mood sentence:** A statistician's working paper set by someone who cares about tooling: ink on white, one deep indigo commitment, numerals in mono because the numbers are the point.

**Color strategy:** Committed. Deep indigo (seed oklch(0.36 0.219 270)) carries the full header band and all interactive color; the body is a pure near-white with near-black ink. Dark scheme follows prefers-color-scheme.
**Type:** tuned system-ui stack (instant load, perfect print) with strong weight/size contrast; ui-monospace reserved for dates and verifiable numerals. No webfonts by choice: a resume is the one brand surface where instant load and clean print are the voice.
**Non-goals:** no blog, no contact form, no analytics beyond none, no framework. One self-contained index.html plus one PDF.
**Print:** printing the page yields a clean paper resume (band flattens to ink-on-white, controls hidden).
